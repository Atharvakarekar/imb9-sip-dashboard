How the numbers are defined

Secured an SIP: the student accepted an offer. Each student holds one accepted SIP.

Opted in: students taking part in the SIP process. Shares use opted-in students; batch strength is shown beside them.

Guest lecture converted: the company sent an SIP JD on or after its guest lecture.

Self-sourced: an SIP a student found outside the committee's process, the "outsourced" box on the whiteboard.

Student-led: self-sourced and case competition SIPs, which no Placecom member owns.

Where the data comes from: the Placement Committee's data sheet, updated by the committee. Companies and guest lectures are tracked by the committee; applications and shortlists come from Infylearn; the student list holds each student's preferred domain and whether they opted in.
