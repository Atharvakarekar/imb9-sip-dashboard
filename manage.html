1Link the Google Sheet

  1. Download the Google Sheet template, upload it to Google Drive, open it and choose File → Save as Google Sheets.
  2. In the Sheet, click Share. Set General access to Anyone with the link → Viewer, and add the teammates who enter data by email as Editors.
  3. Paste the Sheet’s link here and click Connect.

2Share the dashboard

Send this link to anyone. They see the dashboard only: no sign-in and no way to edit.

You opened this page from your computer, so this link only works on your computer. Put the folder online (Netlify or GitHub Pages), open Data & sharing from there, and copy the link again.

    Where the data is stored

    Everything lives in the committee’s Google Sheet in Google Drive. These pages store nothing: they read the Sheet whenever someone opens them and every five minutes after that. In the Sheet, File → Version history shows every change and can undo one.

    Anyone with the dashboard link can also read the Sheet. Use coded student IDs (S001, S002…) and keep the list matching codes to students in a separate private file.
